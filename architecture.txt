CORE DATA FLOW

Instrument Registration
        ↓
Create Evaluation
        ↓
Enter Laboratory & Environmental Conditions
        ↓
Select Applicable OIML R-76 Tests
        ↓
Enter Test Observations
        ↓
Validate Input
        ↓
Apply OIML R-76 Rules
        ↓
Perform Automatic Calculations
        ↓
Determine Test-wise PASS / FAIL
        ↓
Generate Overall Compliance Result
        ↓
Tester Submits Evaluation
        ↓
Reviewer Reviews
        ↓
Approve / Correction Required
        ↓
Generate Standardized Report
        ↓
Store Report
        ↓
Search / Download / Instrument History

IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The system does NOT directly weigh the instrument.

Physical Instrument
        ↓
Human Tester performs test
        ↓
Tester enters observations
        ↓
Software validates data
        ↓
OIML R-76 Rule Engine
        ↓
Calculation Engine
        ↓
Compliance Result
        ↓
PASS / FAIL
        ↓
Standardized Report
